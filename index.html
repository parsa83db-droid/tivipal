Getting started

Up and running in minutes

No typing long URLs with a remote. Add your playlist from your phone or computer and it appears on your screen.

Install tivipal

Get the app on your Samsung or Android TV. It generates a Device ID — tied to your hardware — the first time it opens.

Scan the QR code

Point your phone at the QR code on screen. It opens tivipal.co.uk with your Device ID already filled in.

Push & watch

Add an M3U or Xtream playlist, hit Push. Your device checks it works and loads it — add or remove playlists any time and it follows instantly.

Built-In Optimised Player

Active Connection Counter
